Skeletons are hand-designed (traced from the reference frame), not generated: every stroke oscillates between two parallel direction families, widths are authored per vertex, blades keep ≥5:1 aspect. The variation engine only perturbs what an animator would vary between takes — segment lengths, widths, rotation, mirror, needle length — never the design itself. "Freeform" is the one generated tile, constrained to the same two-family rule. Composition mode rebuilds the reference layout: crown above, jaw pair beside a hollow center, X-crack pair below, needle pair aimed in, green micro-dashes, white center scribble.
